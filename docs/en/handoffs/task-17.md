# Phase 17 — Investigations and conditional impact

Document-ID: EA-HANDOFF-17 · Revision: 1 · Language: en

Prerequisites: the approved completion plan, phase 14 checksummed feature catalog, phase 15 frozen protocol and phase 16 completed development predictions.

Implemented: `investigations.py` provides `build_cases`, `record_review`, `conditional_scenario`, `read_national_margin`, `geographic_sensitivity`, `export_cases_dataset` and `investigations_main`. Case selection verifies the feature/protocol and prediction pins and rejects any test, stress or unknown prediction before publication. Rank both signed directions separately (20 each by default), then hash-sample at most 100 distinct remaining locations round-robin across available UFs. Packets preserve source identities, predictions, descriptive interpretation, alternative explanations and missing independent corroboration. Candidate roles remain blinded; anonymous cycles are never assigned choices. Reviews append immutable referenced case-evidence and investigation records to the phase 15 journal.

Conditional arithmetic supports exact removal, at-most removal and switching caps. Repeated principals and mixed rounds/contests fail rather than double-count. Hypothetical caps and assumed margins are explicitly labeled. A UF result is rejected as a national reference; the optional BR source must carry the official final visible presidential identifiers and matching candidate accounting. Such a source remains a published reference, not an independent recount. Baseline-relative excess uses fixed-valid-turnout expected counts and is never called stolen votes.

Sensitivity resamples entire geographic blocks, defaults to 200 repetitions, and requires a SHA-pinned supplied full-fit/selection callback. It rejects a callback that reports no refitting or selection. Every iteration has a checksummed resumable checkpoint; completed artifacts are verified on reuse. The CLI sensitivity configuration names an explicit local `pipeline_file`, its `pipeline_sha256`, callback `function` (default `full_pipeline`), `blocks`, optional `pipeline_config`, `repetitions` and `seed`. The supplied Python callback receives sampled blocks, seed and pipeline config. Its fit/selection attestations remain supplied execution assertions, not an independent audit of callback correctness. There is no residual-only bootstrap fallback.

Actual AC run: `data/phase17/ac-development-cases` consumed the 399 calibration predictions from `data/phase16/ac-turnout-development`. Published 126 packets: 20 ranked positive, 20 ranked negative and 86 unflagged controls. Only 86 distinct unflagged locations remained after excluding all ranked locations, so the requested 100 is reported honestly. `data/phase17/ac-development-dashboard/dataset.json` is a development-only checksummed exploration export. All 340 protected test units remain excluded. These are candidate-independent turnout model cases, not fraud findings.

Acceptance: five regression tests pass for protected-outcome and changed-source rejection, symmetric ranking/control selection, physical development-only export, immutable reviews, conditional bounds, duplicate/mixed populations, invalid caps, rejection of UF-as-national references, full-pipeline callback requirements and interrupted/resumed checkpoint integrity. Ruff formatting/lint pass. No independent corroboration, national impact estimate, real full-pipeline 200-repeat sensitivity run or automatic fraud conclusion is claimed.

Next: acquire/reconcile national evidence, run every registered model family and validation mechanism, investigate source records and independent corroboration, then perform the frozen final evaluation. New investigation evidence may create new append-only reviews; it cannot retroactively certify initial statistical rankings.
